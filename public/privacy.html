1. Introduction

women-help-api ("we", "our", or "the app") is a menstrual health tracking application designed to help users understand their bodies, track cycles, symptoms, and achieve personal health goals.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data. By using women-help-api, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely)
  • Last period start date

Sign-In Methods

  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive only your name and email address from those services. We do not collect your photo, date of birth, or any other profile information from Google or Apple.

Menstrual & Health Data

  • Period dates, flow, and symptoms
  • Predicted cycles
  • Health notes and goals
  • Cycle history calendar

Profile Picture

  • Optional user-uploaded profile image

Tips & Educational Content

  • Tracking of viewed tips or articles
  • Educational information about menstrual phases

Payments & Subscriptions

women-help-api offers optional subscription plans named:

  • Impact (billed monthly)
  • Impact Plus (billed yearly)
  • Max Impact (billed yearly)

Subscriptions are processed exclusively through the platform associated with your device:

  • Apple App Store (iOS devices) – Payments are processed through your Apple ID account using Apple In-App Purchases.
  • Google Play Store (Android devices) – Payments are processed through your Google account using Google Play Billing.

We do not collect, store, or have direct access to your credit card, debit card, or other payment information. Payment processing, billing details, subscription management, renewals, and refunds are handled directly by Apple or Google in accordance with their respective privacy policies and terms.

We may receive limited information from Apple or Google, such as confirmation of purchase, subscription status, and transaction identifiers, solely for the purpose of activating and managing your subscription within the app.

Charity Contributions

A portion of subscription revenue may be donated to charitable causes. Users may be able to view anonymized impact metrics within the app.

Our app includes a Shop section featuring curated menstrual health products. These products are sourced from Amazon through an affiliate partnership.

When you tap on a product link, you will be redirected to Amazon's website in your browser. At that point, Amazon's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

We may receive a commission for purchases made through these affiliate links. We do not collect, store, or share any data related to your Amazon browsing or purchasing activity.

We only feature products that we believe are relevant and beneficial to our users. Product recommendations are selected by our team and are not influenced by advertising networks.
