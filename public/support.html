Subscriptions & Billing

women-help-api offers optional subscription plans: Impact (billed monthly), Impact Plus (billed yearly), and Max Impact (billed yearly).

  • iOS users subscribe via their Apple ID using Apple In-App Purchases.
  • Android users subscribe via their Google account using Google Play Billing.

All billing, renewals, cancellations, and refund requests must be managed directly through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, according to their respective policies.

Account & Data Deletion Requests

You may delete your account directly within the app at any time.

If you would like to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data, you may submit a request by email.

To request complete data deletion, please email hello@women-help-api.com with the subject line: "Data Deletion Request".

Upon verification of your request, we will permanently delete your personal data from our active systems in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
